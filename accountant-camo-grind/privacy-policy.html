Privacy Policy

Accountant Camo Grind

Effective date: 6 November 2026

JGK Technologies publishes Accountant Camo Grind, an independent weapon-camouflage progression tracker. This policy explains how the application handles information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your weapon levels, camouflage challenge completion, and app preferences locally on your device so that your progress is available when you return. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Collection and sharing

Accountant Camo Grind is designed to work offline. JGK Technologies does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information or your saved camouflage progress to JGK Technologies servers through the app. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not connect to your Activision account. JGK Technologies does not sell your personal information.

Google Play and device services

Google Play and your device's operating system may independently process information such as app installation, purchases, and diagnostic data under their own terms and privacy policies. Those services are not the app's own progress-tracking system. See Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Retention and deletion

Progress and preferences remain on your device until removed through the app or device's data-management functions. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it may permanently delete locally saved progress. The app does not currently provide a JGK Technologies cloud backup or account-based recovery service.

Children's privacy

The app is a gaming progress companion, not a service intended to collect personal information from children. JGK Technologies does not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy may be updated. The effective date above will be revised when changes are published.

Contact

For privacy questions about Accountant Camo Grind, please use the developer support contact shown on its Google Play Store listing.

Accountant Camo Grind is an independent companion app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Activision or Call of Duty.
